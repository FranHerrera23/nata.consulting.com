Services

Planning and construction.

Bring Cuesta in during planning or when you’re ready to build. The team works with owners, architects and trade partners through completion.

Preconstruction

Establish the scope, budget and schedule with the owner and architect before work begins.

Constructability analysis

Review how the design will be built and identify issues that could affect cost or schedule while the team can still address them.

Design-build

Coordinate design and construction through a single contract, bringing in architects and engineers suited to the project.

General contracting

Manage trade partners, field supervision and construction across hospitality, commercial and custom residential projects.

Project management

Coordinate work from planning through completion and keep owners informed during construction.

Historic restoration

Plan renovations around the character of an existing building, as Cuesta did on South Beach hotel projects including Vintro.
