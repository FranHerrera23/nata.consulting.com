Owner’s guide

What should I have ready for a roof inspection?

Start with your property ZIP code, the type of building, what you noticed and when the office can reach you. For a commercial property, include any tenant access or operating hours that could affect the visit.

If water is actively coming in, call the office to discuss urgency.
